💡 What: Optimized Python loops out of hot paths (`data_fetcher.py`, `backtester.py`, `ai_predictor.py`).
🎯 Why: Iterating over DataFrame columns or using `getattr` in `itertuples` was causing significant CPU bottlenecks compared to vectorized Pandas/Numpy operations.
📊 Impact: Expected performance improvement is massive for large datasets or multiple tickers.
🔬 Measurement: Verify by running `python -m pytest tests/` and benchmarking `clean_outliers` or `backtester.run()`.
